    Method — no hardcoded names

    implied = TVL × peerMultiple(category) × momentum(7d) × maturity(age) × breadth(chains) × earlyBonus

    • Peer multiple: median market-cap/TVL of launched protocols in the same category (ratios winsorised to 0.01–3.00; fewer than 3 peers falls back to the global median 0.21×), clamped to 0.02–1.50×.
    • Momentum: the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+120%, mapped to 0.80–1.60×. Missing change counts as 1.00×.
    • Maturity: days since DefiLlama first listed the protocol, mapped to 0.85–1.15× over 0–4 years; missing date counts as 1.00×.
    • Breadth: 1.00× plus 0.05× per extra chain, capped at 1.30×.
    • Early bonus: 1.10× when a protocol listed under a year ago is already growing faster than +10% in 7 days; otherwise 1.00×.
    • Eligible: records no token, and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product, a CEX, or a versioned copy — the snapshot's own watchlist rule, applied to every candidate. All 40 watchlist protocols are on the board.
