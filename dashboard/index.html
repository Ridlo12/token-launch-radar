How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × ratio × momentum × tenure × breadth. Ratio is the category's median market-cap-per-TVL over its tokenised protocols, winsorized at the 5th/95th percentile and shrunk toward the global median (weight 5) — a category with one priced peer is pulled toward the middle instead of trusting the outlier. Momentum is 1 + the 7-day TVL change clamped to −60%/+120%, over 150. Tenure is 1 + 0.45 × √(days listed, capped at 4 years). Breadth is 1 + 0.03 per extra chain, capped at 5. Categories marked thin evidence have two or fewer tokenised peers, so their ratio leans on the global median.
