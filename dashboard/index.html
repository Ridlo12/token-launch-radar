How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × category ratio × momentum × tenure × breadth

  • Category ratio. The median market-cap/TVL of the tokenised protocols in the same category, winsorized at the 5th/95th percentile and shrunk toward the global median with a prior weight of 5 — so one 400× outlier cannot price a whole category, and a category with a single tokenised peer still borrows from the market. A launch is priced the way the market already prices its peers — see the calibration table.
  • Thin evidence. Twenty categories have two or fewer tokenised peers; they are marked thin in the calibration table. Their ratios borrow most of their weight from the global median, so one peer cannot price a whole category alone.
  • Momentum. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, mapped to 0.60…1.80. No reported change counts as flat.
  • Tenure. How long the protocol has run with no token: days since DefiLlama first tracked it, capped at 1,460 days, on a square-root curve to 1.00…1.45. Unreported listing counts as 1.00.
  • Breadth. 1 + 0.03 per extra chain, capped at 5 extra chains.
  • Candidate rule. Ranked only when the snapshot records no token at all, and never a bridge, exchange, liquid-staking product, wrapped or staked product, or a product named for an issuer whose token already trades (Circle, Tether). The 40-protocol watchlist is the floor: all 40 are ranked, plus the 157 eligible protocols below the $50M floor with the most TVL.
  • What the model does not do. A log-log fit of market cap on TVL across the tokenised protocols gives R² 0.19 — too noisy to price from — so the board uses the category medians above, not the fitted curve.
