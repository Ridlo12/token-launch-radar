How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × category ratio × momentum × age × chains

  • Category ratio. The median market-cap/TVL of the tokenised protocols in the same category (at least 3 of them), else the global median of 0.169. A launch is priced the way the market already prices its peers — see the calibration table.
  • Momentum. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, mapped to 0.75…1.50. No reported change counts as flat.
  • Age. Days since DefiLlama first tracked the protocol, capped at 730 days, mapped to 1.00…1.25. A protocol that has run for years with no token has the longer track record.
  • Chains. 1 + 0.03 per extra chain, capped at 5 extra chains.
  • Candidate rule. Ranked only when the snapshot records no token at all, and never a bridge, exchange, liquid-staking product, wrapped or staked product, or versioned deployment. The 40-protocol watchlist is the floor: all 40 are ranked, plus the 158 eligible protocols below the $50M floor with the most TVL.
